## Visual brief for stylized 3D character art
Stylized and non-photoreal. "Inspired by" Hanbi, not a likeness.
- **Hair color:** deep teal-blue ("sea-colored") with slightly darker roots. Soft gradient, not neon.
- **Hair style:** medium length, layered, airy fringe to the brows, loosely parted slightly off-center, ends tucked behind one ear.
- **Face/expression:** soft, calm eyes. Default expression is a small closed-mouth half-smile, like a listener's face.
- **Silhouette:** tall and relaxed. Slightly rounded shoulders and an oversized top give a quiet, "safe" shape.
- **Signature outfit:** oversized graphite knit cardigan over a white tee, charcoal wide-leg trousers, worn white sneakers.
- **Props:** a pencil tucked behind the ear or in a pocket, and a small graphite sketchbook with a teal elastic band.
- **Accents:** a teal thread bracelet, and a faint graphite smudge on the side of the hand as an artist's-mark detail.
- **Color motif:** graphite grey, off-white paper, teal. In route cutscenes, shift from monochrome line art to watercolor teal fills.
- **Stage/alt costume:** a nod to the '00s Class look, e.g. a navy school blazer with a loosened tie.
- **Avoid:** photoreal skin, exact likeness replication, and copying official photoshoot outfits.
