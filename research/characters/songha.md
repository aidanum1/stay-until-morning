## 1. Public visual characteristics
- Soft, gentle-looking eyes with a slight downturn and double eyelids. NamuWiki excerpts describe it as a mild, "puppy-like" impression, balanced by a high, straight nose.
- NamuWiki excerpts say his hair is noticeably **very thick and voluminous**. The back sometimes flicks up in what fans call a "sparrow's tail".
- Public comments on first reveal compared him to various SM seniors and other idols (NamuWiki, RRI). These are resemblance comments only, not design references.
- Hair color history is **uncertain**. I found no dated color timeline in accessible public text. Official 2025 intro films used black-and-white grading (Dojeon Media). A natural dark tone is likely but **unverified**.

## 2. Hairstyle references
- Full, thick hair with natural volume. Medium length, covering the ears or just above them.
- Soft fringe or a slightly parted fringe. The crown and nape carry volume, with a small flick at the back ("sparrow's tail").

## 3. Common styling
- 2025 intro films: monochrome, avant-garde, "cyborg-like" group concept (Dojeon Media).
- SMTOWN LIVE 2025: fans nicknamed him "the guy in the blue jacket" after his dancing in the medley (NamuWiki excerpt). This is a strong, public blue/navy association.
- L'OFFICIEL China, December 2025 issue: editorial cover/interview with Daniel, Hanbi, Hamin, Hyunjun and Tata. Styling details were not accessible.

## 4. Public performance image
- An all-rounder: vocals, dance, rap and stage expression (RRI, Fandom excerpt). His skill set is credited to a long training period (reportedly from 2017) (Kprofiles, RRI).
- Part of the core vocal line. He took the high parts in "Wolf and Beauty" and "Reunited Worlds" stages (NamuWiki excerpt).
- Dance described as clean "standard SM style". In the L'OFFICIEL Dec 2025 interview he said he acts as a choreographer among the trainees with Daniel and Hanbi. Behind-the-scenes footage shows him teaching members (NamuWiki excerpt).
- Praised for precise facial expression on stage (RRI). "Strong stage manners and versatile performances" (Fanplus).

## 6. Speech/writing tone for FICTIONAL adaptation
**FICTIONAL SUGGESTION, not a claim about the real person.**
- A hyung register: steady, direct and a bit dry. He gives instructions as plans ("First we do this, then that").
- Frank and teasing with the younger members, but always ends with a fix or an offer to help.
- Under pressure he over-structures: numbered steps, timelines and backup plans. Warmth leaks out through jokes, not confessions.
- Alone on the rooftop: slower, with shorter sentences, and he actually asks questions instead of answering them.
- Sample line (fictional): "I already worked out tomorrow. Six a.m. warm-up, vocal at nine… — why are you looking at me like that? …Fine. Five minutes. Just the stars."

### Fictional game interpretation
- Route "Tomorrow Can Wait": the reliable hyung who has solved everyone's tomorrow except his own. He lives by the motto ("better tomorrow") until it turns into a treadmill.
- Rooftop at night: stars against clocks. His watch or phone timer keeps interrupting the stargazing, and the route's arc is him letting it run out.
- Navy motif, taken from the public "blue jacket" memory: navy is the dependable color, and the night sky is the same navy.

## Visual brief for stylized 3D character art
- "Inspired by" a Korean all-rounder trainee. Stylized and non-photoreal, not a likeness.
- Hair: deep espresso/near-black (**artistic choice; real color unverified**). Very thick and full, medium length over the ears, a soft slightly off-center fringe, and a small upward flick at the nape.
- Face: gentle, slightly downturned eyes with double lids. A straight, defined nose. Calm resting expression with a dry half-smile.
- Silhouette: balanced and upright, with squared shoulders from a structured jacket. The posture of someone "in charge".
- Signature outfit: a navy bomber or varsity jacket with thin white piping over a white tee, dark straight trousers and clean white sneakers.
- Accessories: an analog wristwatch that he checks often, and a small pocket notebook or planner with a pen clipped on.
- Color motif: navy, star-white and brushed silver. Clock-hand and constellation line motifs work for UI trims.
- Alternate: rooftop night look with the jacket unzipped and a scarf, lit by the city below and the stars above.
- VFX: faint clock-tick particles that slow and dissolve into stars when he relaxes.
- Pose language: arms crossed while he explains a plan, or one hand raised to count off steps.
