## 1. Public visual characteristics
- Tall and lean. Profile sites list him at about 180 cm, which gives a long-limbed dancer's line.
- NamuWiki describes his look as mixed Japanese/American heritage. For the artist, that means soft but defined features that don't follow one "type".
- Hair color history is **uncertain**. I could not verify a dated color timeline from public text. Fan search snippets mention "long brown hair" at some point, date unknown. ## 2. Hairstyle references

- Stage history: SMTOWN LIVE 2025 debut stage (11 Jan 2025) with covers of EXO's "Growl" and SHINee's "Lucifer" (KpopStarz). He also appears on the 2026 single "We Go Up" (Korea Daily, Hitkultr).

### Fictional game interpretation
- Route "No Audience": the bright persona is real, but it's also a performance. Alone in an empty practice room at night, with mirrors on every wall, he dances for no one and asks who he is when the reflections stop clapping.

- Color motif: white, red and graphite. Red and white light-trail VFX on limbs during dance.
- Prop: a small teddy-bear keychain (nod to the fan emoji). Optional guitar pick on a chain.
- Environment: mirrored practice room, fluorescent strip lights, reflections running a half-beat delayed.
