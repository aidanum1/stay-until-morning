- *Inspired by* Haruta of SMTR25. Stylized, non-photoreal, and not a likeness.
- Hair: **the real color is uncertain.** Game choice: soft dark brown with a subtle violet sheen under cinema light.
- Style: a neat, clean cut, medium-short, with a soft side-swept fringe and a light side part. Tidy, but with one stray strand that hints at his hidden playfulness.
- Expression default: a calm, gentle half-smile. His playful face is a sudden, bright scrunch.
- Silhouette: slim and composed, with upright posture. His elegance comes through in how neat he looks.
- Signature outfit: a crisp white shirt and a charcoal cardigan, with a violet-and-grey '10s Ujeong High tie worn neatly.
- Off-duty variant: a long muted-violet coat over a knit, like an old usher's jacket.
- Accessory: a vintage ticket stub tucked in his pocket, or a small film-reel pin.
- Color motif: violet, dusk lavender, sepia and warm projector gold.
- Lighting: flickering projector beam, film-grain overlay in his route CGs.
