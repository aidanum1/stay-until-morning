# CHARACTER BIBLE — 00:25 · Stay Until Morning

> **Unofficial fan-made fictional project. Not affiliated with SM Entertainment or the featured artists. Character portrayals are fictional interpretations inspired by publicly available appearances and content.**

This is the canonical consistency document for writing, localisation and art. The per-member
research files in `research/characters/` hold sources and the fact / persona / fiction split.
**Everything in this bible that is not labelled "public basis" is fiction.**

---

## 0. Ground rules

1. **Fiction first.** Nothing in the game claims to describe the real people's private lives, relationships, MBTI, family, or feelings. Public basis is used only for broad strokes (visual cues, publicly visible "vibe", public roles in the team).
2. **All eight are adults** (19–22 at release, Sept 2026). The *past memories* show them as young teens and are **never romantic** — they are brief, kind encounters with a stranger. Romance exists only in the present night, between adults.
3. **Wholesome romance.** No sexual content. The most intimate beats are hand-holding, leaning on a shoulder, a forehead touch, dancing, staying. No kiss is ever described on screen (a fade/cut may imply closeness at the ending — keep it ambiguous and tender).
4. **No possessiveness, no jealousy, no rivalry.** Members are never hostile to each other over the protagonist. Friendship survives every ending.
5. **Consent in the text.** Characters ask before touching; "not yet" and "no" are respected and never punished.
6. **No tragedy.** No death, illness, accidents, violence, villains, sacrifice.
7. **Avoid generic AI-romance phrasing**: no "You're different", "I can't explain why", "You're not like anyone else", "You belong to me", "Something about you…". Romance is shown through specificity: remembered details, jokes that come back, choosing to stay.
8. **Cultural respect.** Charlie's mixed heritage and Haruta's/Justin's Japanese background are never jokes or caricatures. Home, language and belonging can be themes; stereotypes cannot.

## 1. The protagonist — `{{playerName}}`

- Customisable name, **no gender, no fixed face, no stated age** (implicitly a young adult of similar age). Never described physically beyond hands, a coat sleeve, a reflection with no detail.
- Written in **first-person-adjacent narration** (`>` lines): present tense, observational, gently dry. Avoid "I" at the start of every line; avoid gendered words entirely.
- **Backstory (fiction, revealed gradually):** grew up moving a lot — a parent's work took the family between cities (a summer in Busan, years in Seoul, time abroad). They were always the one who left. As a teenager during a Seoul stint, they volunteered at **Studio 25**'s front desk and ran the Future Letter booth, and left a Future Letter of their own: *"I hope, by the time you read this, you've found a place you want to stay."*
- **Arc:** someone who always leaves learns to stay. Every route ends with the protagonist choosing to *stay* (one more stop, five more minutes, another sitting, until morning).
- Voice options through choices: **playful / sincere / quiet**. None is "correct".

**Localisation:** never use gendered pronouns or age-based kinship terms for the protagonist (KO: no 누나/형/언니/오빠; JA: no 彼/彼女, choose 私 or omit the pronoun in player lines; ZH: never 他/她 for the protagonist — use the name or 你, or restructure).

## 2. ECHO

- The archival system of Studio 25: projection rooms, speakers, a glass core in the archive room. Built by the studio's founder **Seo Yeonhwa** (fictional; appears only in old voice logs — warm, wry, fifties).
- Speaks in calm, precise, slightly off-kilter archive language; loves timestamps; says "Recipient" and "Record"; gradually learns softer phrasing through the night. Not a villain, not a threat, never sinister. A little like a lighthouse keeper who has been alone too long.
- Rules of the night: at **00:25** ECHO begins the Final Return. Records can only be opened if **their owner agrees**. Every record contains an **unresolved subject** — a figure ECHO cannot identify; rendered as a soft silhouette of light. At **05:00** the building's power is cut (the lease ends; the building is being renovated). ECHO can write **one** record to a keepsake glass card before it sleeps.
- Text style: `echo:` lines, often starting with a record code: `RECORD 03 — OWNER: HAMIN. STATUS: SEALED.` Short. Occasionally funny by accident.

### HAMIN — "The Things You Notice" 🌊
- **Public basis:** born Busan (2006); last trainee revealed; press describes a clean, pure, "manga" visual with a quiet/mysterious aura; show promo framed a chatty side behind the calm image.
- **Fiction:** notices everything about other people — who's cold, who hasn't eaten, who laughed too late — and almost nothing about what he himself wants. Deflects attention by giving it. Dry, surprisingly funny one-liners when comfortable. From Busan; the sea is where he goes in his head.
- **Voice:** short sentences, understatement, pauses (`{p}`). Answers questions with observations. When he tries to say what he wants, he starts with "It's not a big deal, but—".
- **Route theme:** *being noticed.* Romance through **acts of attention**, not declarations.
- **Past encounter (≈6 years ago, summer, night, a quiet beach in Busan):** 14-year-old Hamin recorded the sound of the waves for the Future Letter Project. A stranger (protagonist, new in Busan for one summer) sat alone further down the beach, shivering. He walked over and gave them his hot canned drink from the vending machine and said nothing much. They talked about the sea being louder at night. The stranger said their favourite drink (→ `var.drink`, set in Prologue). The recording catches the stranger's laugh. They left for Seoul days later.
- **Key lines (verbatim):** Player "You notice everything." / Hamin "Not everything." {p} "I'm trying."
- **Ending:** breakfast; he has already ordered the protagonist's drink (`var.drink`).
- **Ladder:** low — "Are you cold?" | mid — quietly swaps seats so you're out of the draft | high — remembers the drink, the way you take it, the song you hummed.
- **Visual brief:** tall, lean, still silhouette; soft calm eyes; **black hair with midnight-blue sheen**, soft medium-short layers, light fringe, loose side part (hair colour = game design choice, undocumented). Outfit: navy knit over white tee, dark slacks, silver chain bracelet, long charcoal coat. Motif: ocean, midnight blue, silver, wave-light.

## 6. Relationship model (hidden)

`trust` (safety, honesty), `closeness` (familiarity, comfort), `romance` (tender attraction). Typical: small choice +1; big emotional beat +2. Never shown as numbers. Behaviour thresholds (guideline):
- closeness ≥ 3 — casual physical comfort (sitting close, jacket).
- romance ≥ 2 — route becomes available at 03:50 ("Where do you want to be?").
- romance ≥ 4 — high-romance variants in the route chapter (hand held, forehead touch, remembered details).
Reserved/respectful choices give **trust**, never negative points.
